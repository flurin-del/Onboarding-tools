How to add your signature to Gmail

1

Open Gmail settings

Click the gear icon ⚙ in the top-right corner of Gmail.

2

Open all settings

In the quick settings panel, click See all settings.

3

Find the Signature section

Scroll down the General tab to Signature. Click Create new, paste your signature, then save.

4

Send a test email

Save changes and send a quick test to matthias@lightly.ai to confirm everything looks right.
